Whānau School Planner

A private planner one family uses to organise school drop-offs, pick-ups and before- and after-school care.

What it is

It reads the family's own calendars and work roster, suggests who takes the children to school and who collects them, and writes the agreed plan back to the family's own Google Calendars.

It is not a public service. Only two Google accounts belonging to the family can sign in, and there is no way to sign up.

Privacy

How the planner handles information is set out in its privacy policy.
