Privacy policy

Last updated 18/09/2026.

Who uses it

The Whānau School Planner is used privately by one family. Only a short list of Google accounts can sign in: the family's own, and a view-only account for the whānau they share school runs with. It has no other users and does not accept sign-ups.

What it reads

  • Your Google account email, when you sign in, to check you are one of the allowed accounts. Nothing else is taken from your Google profile.
  • Google Calendars the family owns or has been given access to, to work out who is available.
  • Google Drive files the planner itself created, to file leave applications.
  • A work roster the family has been given access to.
  • Google Maps travel times between the family's usual places.

What it writes

Only the family's own Google Calendars, and only events the planner itself created. It never changes or deletes anybody else's events, and never writes to a calendar it was only given permission to read.

Where information is kept

On the family's own Google Cloud server and in the family's own Google Calendars. Nothing is sold, shared with anybody else, or used for advertising. No analytics or tracking is used.

The planner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing information

Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google account's third-party connections page. To have anything the planner holds deleted, email the contact below.
